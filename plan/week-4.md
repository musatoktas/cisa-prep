# Week 4: Operations and Business Resilience (26%)

Goal: the day-to-day running of IT and how the organisation survives disruption. This is a quarter of the exam.

| Day | Topic | Read | Practise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Change, configuration and patch management; emergency changes | CRM Domain 4, Part A | 45 Qs on change management |
| Tue | Operational log management, service level management, database management (integrity, backup, concurrency) | CRM Domain 4, Part A | 45 Qs on operations |
| Wed | Business impact analysis, RTO, RPO, MTD, criticality | CRM Domain 4, Part B; ISO 22301 overview | 45 Qs on BIA |
| Thu | System and operational resilience: redundancy, clustering, RAID, high availability | CRM Domain 4, Part B | 45 Qs on resilience |
| Fri | Backup types (full, incremental, differential), storage, rotation, restoration testing | CRM Domain 4, Part B | 45 Qs on backup and restore |
| Sat | Business continuity plans and disaster recovery plans: recovery sites (hot, warm, cold, mirrored), plan testing (checklist, tabletop, simulation, parallel, full interruption) | CRM Domain 4, Part B | 45 Qs on BCP and DRP |
| Sun | Review | Your wrong-answer log | 50 mixed Qs from Domains 1 to 4 |

## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- Why the BIA comes before the recovery strategy, and who signs it off
- RPO drives backup frequency; RTO drives the recovery site and method
- Incremental vs differential backup: which is faster to take, which is faster to restore
- The most important test of a backup (a successful restore)
- Which BCP test type is the least disruptive and which is the most realistic
- Why emergency changes still need documentation and retrospective approval
- The difference between an incident and a problem
