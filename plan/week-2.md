## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- The board sets direction and is accountable; management executes. Which answers put responsibility in the wrong place?
- Why data owners, not IT, classify data and approve access
- Incompatible duties and the compensating controls when segregation is not possible in a small team
- What a right-to-audit clause gives you, and what to rely on when you cannot audit a provider directly (independent assurance reports such as SOC 2 Type 2)
- The difference between a KPI and a KRI
- How an IT strategy is shown to be aligned with the business strategy
