# Week 6: Mock exams, weak areas, exam day

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| Mon | **Mock exam 1**: 150 questions, 4 hours, exam conditions (no phone, one break). Score it by domain |
| Tue | Review every question from mock 1. Rank the domains by score. Spend the remaining time on the weakest domain's CRM chapter |
| Wed | 4 hours on the two weakest domains: 60 min reading, 120 min questions, 60 min review |
| Thu | **Mock exam 2**: 150 new questions, exam conditions |
| Fri | Review mock 2. Re-read your one-page summaries for all five domains. Re-do every question in the wrong-answer log |
| Sat | Light day: 1 hour of flashcards, 50 mixed questions, check exam logistics (ID, time, route or remote setup) |
| Sun | Rest, or exam day if booked for the weekend |

## Ready or not

You are probably ready when:

- Both mock exams are at **75% or higher**
- No single domain is below **65%**
- Your wrong-answer log shows the same mistake fewer than twice

If not, move the exam by a week or two. Rescheduling rules and fees are in the ISACA candidate guide. A week of extra study is cheaper than a retake.
