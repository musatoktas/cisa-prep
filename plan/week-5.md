# Week 5: Protection of Information Assets (26%)

Goal: security controls and how to audit them. If you come from ISO/IEC 27001, Annex A maps closely to most of this week.

| Day | Topic | Read | Practise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Security policies and frameworks, physical and environmental controls | CRM Domain 5, Part A; ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A overview | 45 Qs on policy and physical security |

| Wed | Network and endpoint security: firewalls, segmentation, IDS and IPS, VPN, wireless, mobile and IoT | CRM Domain 5, Part A | 45 Qs on network security |
| Thu | Encryption (symmetric, asymmetric, hashing), digital signatures, PKI, data loss prevention, cloud and virtualisation | CRM Domain 5, Part A | 45 Qs on cryptography and cloud |

| Sun | Review | Your wrong-answer log | 50 mixed Qs from all domains |

## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- Which key encrypts and which key verifies in a digital signature, and what a signature proves
- Why hashing gives integrity but not confidentiality
- Who should approve access, who should grant it, and who should review it
- The most effective control against a terminated employee's access
- The first step in incident response, and why evidence preservation comes before system rebuild
- What a penetration tester needs before starting (written authorisation and agreed scope)
- Shared responsibility in cloud services, and what the customer still owns
