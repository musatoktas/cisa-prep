# The 6-week plan

| Week | Focus | Target by Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 1](week-1.md) | Domain 1: Information System Auditing Process | 250 questions done, 70%+ on Domain 1 |
| [Week 2](week-2.md) | Domain 2: Governance and Management of IT | 250 questions done, 70%+ on Domain 2 |
| [Week 3](week-3.md) | Domain 3, then the start of Domain 4 | 250 questions done, 70%+ on Domain 3 |
| [Week 4](week-4.md) | Domain 4: Operations and Business Resilience | 250 questions done, 70%+ on Domain 4 |
| [Week 5](week-5.md) | Domain 5: Protection of Information Assets | 250 questions done, 70%+ on Domain 5 |
| [Week 6](week-6.md) | Two full mock exams, weak areas, exam | 75%+ on both mocks |

Roughly 1,300 to 1,500 practice questions in total. That is about the size of the ISACA QAE database, so by week 6 you will have seen most of it once and your weak areas twice.

## Daily shape (Monday to Saturday)

| Block | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Read | 60 min | The day's topic in the CISA Review Manual or the linked standard |
| Condense | 45 min | One-page summary in your own words, 10 flashcards |
| Practise | 90 min | 40 to 50 timed questions on the day's topic |
| Review | 45 min | Read every explanation, log every miss in the tracker |

## Sunday

2 hours: 50 mixed questions from every domain finished so far, then a pass through the wrong-answer log. Rest the other 2 hours. Fatigue costs more marks than a missed topic.

## If you fall behind

Do not skip the practice block. Cut reading time first, practice time last. Questions show you what the exam actually asks, and the explanations teach the reading you skipped.
