# Week 1: Information System Auditing Process (18%)

Goal: think like an auditor. Every later domain is tested through this lens, so this week pays off across the whole exam.

| Day | Topic | Read | Practise |

| Wed | Types of controls (preventive, detective, corrective, compensating), control objectives, control self-assessment | CRM Domain 1, Part A, controls section | 40 Qs on control types |

| Sun | Review | Your wrong-answer log | 50 mixed Domain 1 Qs |

## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- Why the audit charter matters and who approves it
- Inherent risk, control risk, detection risk and how they combine into audit risk
- The difference between a compliance test and a substantive test, and which comes first
- When to use statistical sampling, and what confidence level and tolerable error do to sample size
- What makes evidence reliable: source independence, the auditor's own observation, documented over oral
- What to do when an auditee disagrees with a finding
- What to do when you find something outside the audit scope
