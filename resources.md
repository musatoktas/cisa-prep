# Resources

Prices, editions and availability change. Check the publisher before buying.

## Official ISACA (the core)

| Resource | Why | How to use it in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| **CISA Exam Content Outline** (free, isaca.org) | The definitive list of what the exam covers | Read on day 1. Tick off each task statement as you study it |
| **CISA Exam Candidate Guide** (free, isaca.org) | Booking, ID rules, remote proctoring, rescheduling, scoring | Read before you book |
| **CISA Review Manual (CRM)** | The official study text, organised by domain | The "Read" block every day |
| **CISA Review Questions, Answers and Explanations (QAE) database** | The official practice question bank, with explanations and exam simulation | The "Practise" block every day and both mock exams in week 6. If you buy only one thing, buy this |
| **ISACA membership** | Lower exam and resource prices, the ISACA Journal, local chapter events | Join before you register for the exam if the saving covers the fee |
| **ISACA Glossary** (free) | ISACA's own definitions, which the exam follows | Look up any term you are unsure of |

## Free and low-cost extras

- **ISACA Journal** articles on audit topics: good for real examples of controls in practice
- **ISACA local chapter study groups**: many chapters run CISA review sessions; check your local chapter
- **Video courses**: several instructors publish CISA courses on mainstream learning platforms. Check that the course matches the current exam content outline before buying

## What to avoid

- **Exam dumps** or sites that claim to have real exam questions. Using them breaks the ISACA candidate agreement and can cost you the certification
- **Old editions** of the CRM or QAE. Domain structure and weights change between job practice updates
- Studying only from one video course. The exam tests judgement, which comes from many explained questions
