# Exam day

Check every detail here against the current ISACA Exam Candidate Guide. Rules change.

## Booking

- Register and pay on isaca.org. You then get an eligibility window to schedule the exam with PSI.
- Book the date before week 1. A fixed date keeps the plan honest.
- Note the deadline for rescheduling without a fee.

## Test centre or remote?

| | Test centre | Remote proctoring |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Nothing to prepare | Webcam, microphone, PSI secure browser, stable internet |
| Room | Provided | Private, quiet room, clear desk, nobody entering for 4 hours |
| Risk | Travel time | Connection drops, room interruptions, failed system check |
| Best for | Anyone without a guaranteed quiet room and reliable connection | People with a quiet home office and wired or very stable internet |

If you choose remote, run PSI's system check on the same computer and network you will use on the day, at least a week before.

## The day before

- Light review only: flashcards and your one-page summaries
- Confirm your ID matches your registration name exactly
- Sleep. A tired brain misreads *least* as *most*

## In the exam

- **Pace**: 150 questions in 240 minutes is 1.6 minutes per question. Check the clock at question 50 (about 80 minutes) and question 100 (about 160 minutes)
- **Read the last line first**: find the qualifier (*first*, *best*, *most*, *primary*), then read the scenario
- **Eliminate**: remove the options where the auditor acts as management, loses independence, or acts before understanding risk
- **Flag and move on**: do not spend more than 3 minutes on one question. Come back at the end
- **Do not change answers without a reason**: change only when you notice a word you misread

## After the exam

- You usually see a preliminary pass or fail on screen. The official score follows by email
- After passing, apply for certification with your experience verification within the window set by ISACA
- Start earning CPE hours. Keep a simple log from day one
